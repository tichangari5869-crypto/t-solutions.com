Refund Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. This is a plain language draft; have a Kenyan advocate review it before launch.

Pay after you receive

You pay only after you receive your work, so most orders need no refund.

If something is wrong

Tell us on WhatsApp within 3 days of receiving your work. We will redo it free if the mistake is ours.

Refunds

If we cannot do the service, you pay nothing. If you already paid and we cannot fix a fault, we refund the amount for that item by M-Pesa.

Custom prints

Custom items are made for you, so we cannot refund a change of mind after printing. A misprint or damage on our side is reprinted free.

Third party fees

Government and portal fees are paid to them and are not refundable by us.
